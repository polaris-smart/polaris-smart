# Polaris-Smart LAB · 北极星智能实验室

**Local-first AI collaboration infrastructure.** We build tools that let humans manage AI agents like a real team — with accountability, evidence chains, and human review at every critical step.

> Every task delegated, every deliverable submitted, every review decision — recorded, auditable, on your own machine.

## 🔭 Main Projects

| Project | What it does |
|---|---|
| [agent-mailbox](https://github.com/polaris-smart/agent-mailbox) | Local-first team workbench for AI agents: project mailboxes, shared context, task handoffs, human review. Think DingTalk for AI employees. |

## 🧭 Principles

- **Local-first** — your data and evidence stay on your machine; no mandatory cloud.
- **Evidence chains** — who assigned, who accepted, what was delivered, who verified. Every step leaves a receipt.
- **Human at the gate** — agents work autonomously; humans review what matters.
- **Ship, then polish** — local versions lead public releases by 1–2 minor versions.
